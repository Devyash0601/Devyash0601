### 👨‍💻 About Me

I build systems at the intersection of **published research** and **production ML engineering** — agentic AI workflows, Small Language Model (SLM) fine-tuning, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and high-resolution geospatial deep learning. 

- 🎓 **Education**: B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning at **BIT Mesra**.
- 💼 **Current Role**: AI/ML Intern at **Wipro**, fine-tuning SLMs and developing agentic RAG architectures.
- 🔬 **Research**: Published first-author paper on weakly-supervised generative models for geospatial mapping (**ECCS 2026**).
- 🎯 **Looking For**: MSc research opportunities/collaborations in AI/ML across Europe and high-impact ML engineering roles.

---

### 📄 Research & Publications

- **Adaptive WGAST: Weakly-Supervised Generative Model for Geospatial Mapping**
  - *Published at European Conference on Communication Systems (ECCS 2026)* — **First Author**
  - Designed an adaptive generative framework featuring Adaptive Denoising Blocks (ADB) and Spatial Feature Refinement (SFR) modules for high-resolution satellite thermal reconstruction.
