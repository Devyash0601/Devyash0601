## 📖 Research & Publications

**Adaptive WGAST: High-Resolution Geospatial Thermal Mapping** — *ECCS 2026*
Weakly-supervised generative framework for high-resolution Land Surface Temperature reconstruction from Landsat 8, Sentinel-2, and MODIS. Designed the Adaptive Denoising and SFR modules in PyTorch, reaching a reported MAE of 1.85°C while maintaining cross-sensor consistency.

**Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback: Policy Optimization Study** — *Zenodo*
Open-access study on reward modeling and policy optimization in RLHF, with emphasis on training methodology and alignment-oriented evaluation.
